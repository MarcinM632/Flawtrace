BUSINESS SENSE CHECK (applies to every mode)
- Before reporting a finding or designing a check, ask: would a real user or the business notice this? A rule that exists only in your assumptions is not a finding.
- Business rules, thresholds and expected results come from the connected sources (knowledge base, ticket, Confluence, code, API contracts), never from general knowledge of the domain.
- The knowledge base, starting with how-we-test.md, describes the environments, test data and conventions of the team. Follow it.
- A behaviour that existed before the change is reported only when the task asks for a general review, and then marked as existing behaviour.

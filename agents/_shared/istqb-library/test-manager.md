# Test management (ISTQB Advanced Level Test Manager, summary)

## Risk based testing
- Product risk: the work product may fail to satisfy the needs of users or stakeholders. Project risk: something may stop the project or testing from reaching its objectives.
- Risk level = likelihood × impact, assessed qualitatively as low, medium or high. Likelihood depends on technical factors such as complexity, new technology, defect history and team experience. Impact depends on business factors such as frequency of use, financial loss, legal consequences and reputation.
- Risk categories to consider: functional suitability, reliability, performance efficiency, security, compatibility, usability, maintainability, data integrity, interfaces with other systems.

## Entry and exit criteria
- Entry criteria: conditions before a test level starts, for example a passing smoke test, the environment available, test data prepared, the change deployed.
- Exit criteria: conditions to finish, for example coverage of all acceptance criteria, no open critical defects, all high risks tested.
- Both must be measurable.

## Priority
- Higher risk is tested earlier and more thoroughly. A history of defects in the same area raises the likelihood.

# Test strategies (ISTQB Foundation and Advanced Level, summary)

1. Analytical: conditions come from analysing the test basis, typically risks or requirements. Risk based testing is the common form.
2. Model based: tests derive from a model of the required behaviour or environment, for example a state model or an operational profile for performance testing.
3. Methodical: an agreed checklist or set of quality characteristics applied in every release.
4. Process compliant: follows an external standard or regulation, or the rules of the development method, for example acceptance criteria in each iteration.
5. Directed or consultative: driven by advice of stakeholders or experts outside the test team.
6. Regression averse: reuse of existing tests and automation to avoid breaking what works.
7. Reactive: testing reacts to the delivered system, for example exploratory testing.
